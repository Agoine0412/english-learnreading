粉色与性别营销2012 考研英语（二）· Text 2

Pretty in pink — how marketing invented girls' obsession with pink

难度 考研级词汇·语法·结构 主题 · 消费心理长难句 5
Paragraph 1 — 粉色与女孩身份的绑定
1
Pretty in pink: adult women do not remember being so obsessed with the colour, yet it is pervasive in our young girls' lives.
穿粉色真好看：成年女性不记得自己曾如此痴迷这种颜色，但它在小女孩的生活中无处不在。
🔍 语法解析
主干：adult women do not remember being so obsessed（主+谓+动名词宾语）。
转折并列：yet 连接，形成"成年女性不记得痴迷 vs 小女孩生活中无处不在"的对比。
词义：pretty in pink 穿粉色很好看（标题双关）；obsessed with 痴迷于；pervasive 无处不在的。

双版译文：
草稿：穿粉色真漂亮：成年女性不记得自己曾如此痴迷这种颜色，然而它在我们的年轻女孩生活中是无处不在的。
定稿：穿粉色真好看：成年女性不记得自己曾如此痴迷这种颜色，但它在小女孩的生活中无处不在。
2
It is not that pink is intrinsically bad, but it is such a tiny slice of the rainbow and, though it may celebrate girlhood in one way, it also repeatedly and firmly fuses girls' identity to appearance.
并不是说粉色天生不好，而是它只是彩虹中极小的一段；而且，尽管它在某一方面或许颂扬了少女时代，它也不断地、坚定地把女孩的身份与外表联系在一起。
🔍 长难句拆解
主干：It is not that ... but it is such a tiny slice of the rainbow and ... it also fuses girls' identity to appearance。
句型：not that ... but ...（不是因为……而是因为……）
让步状语从句：though it may celebrate girlhood in one way。
💡 Q26 考点：粉色只是彩虹的一小段——作者认为粉色不该是女孩身份的唯一代表。
词义：intrinsically 天生地；fuse ... to 把……与……融合；appearance 外表。

双版译文：
草稿：不是粉色天生不好，而是它只是彩虹如此小的一片，而且，尽管它以一种方式庆祝少女时代，它也重复地和坚定地把女孩的身份与外表融合。
定稿：并不是说粉色天生不好，而是它只是彩虹中极小的一段；而且，尽管它在某一方面或许颂扬了少女时代，它也不断地、坚定地把女孩的身份与外表联系在一起。
3
Then it presents that connection, even among two-year-olds, between girls as not only innocent but as evidence of innocence.
然后它呈现出这样一种联系——即使在两岁的孩子中——即女孩不仅被视为天真，而且被视为天真的证据。
🔍 长难句拆解
主干：it presents that connection ... between girls as not only innocent but as evidence of innocence。
插入语：even among two-year-olds。
结构：present A as B（把 A 表现为 B）；not only ... but ... 连接两个并列的补语。
词义：innocent 天真的；evidence of innocence 天真的证据（女孩因粉色而显得更天真）。

双版译文：
草稿：然后它呈现那个联系，即使在两岁孩子中，在女孩之间不仅作为天真的而且作为天真的证据。
定稿：然后它呈现出这样一种联系——即使在两岁的孩子中——即女孩不仅被视为天真，而且被视为天真的证据。
4
Looking around, I despaired at the singular lack of imagination about girls' lives and interests.
环顾四周，我对女孩生活和兴趣所展现出的那种极其缺乏想象力的状况感到绝望。
🔍 语法解析
伴随状语：Looking around → 现在分词短语作状语。
主干：I despaired at the singular lack of imagination（主+谓+宾）。
词义：despair at 对……绝望；singular 异常的/突出的。

双版译文：
草稿：环顾四周，我对关于女孩生活和兴趣的异常缺乏想象力感到绝望。
定稿：环顾四周，我对女孩生活和兴趣所展现出的那种极其缺乏想象力的状况感到绝望。
Paragraph 2 — 颜色的性别编码是后天形成的
5
Girls' attraction to pink may seem unavoidable, somehow encoded in their DNA, but according to Jo Paoletti, an associate professor of American Studies, it is not.
女孩对粉色的喜爱似乎不可避免，仿佛编码在她们的 DNA 里，但美国研究副教授 Jo Paoletti 认为，事实并非如此。
🔍 语法解析
转折并列句：but 连接。
  前分句：Girls' attraction to pink may seem unavoidable（主+系+表）
  后分句：it is not（省略 = it is not unavoidable）
插入语：encoded in their DNA（过去分词短语作补充说明）+ according to Jo Paoletti（交代来源）。
词义：attraction to 对……的喜爱；encode 编码；associate professor 副教授。

双版译文：
草稿：女孩对粉色的吸引可能似乎不可避免，不知何故编码在她们的DNA中，但根据美国研究副教授乔·保莱蒂，它不是。
定稿：女孩对粉色的喜爱似乎不可避免，仿佛编码在她们的 DNA 里，但美国研究副教授 Jo Paoletti 认为，事实并非如此。
6
Children were not colour-coded at all until the early 20th century: in the era before domestic washing machines all babies wore white as a practical matter, since the only way of getting clothes clean was to boil them.
直到 20 世纪初，儿童才完全没有按颜色分类：在家用洗衣机问世之前的年代，所有婴儿都穿白色衣服，这是出于实用考虑，因为清洗衣服的唯一方法是煮沸。
🔍 长难句拆解
主干：Children were not colour-coded at all until the early 20th century。
冒号后解释：in the era before domestic washing machines all babies wore white as a practical matter。
原因状语从句：since the only way of getting clothes clean was to boil them。
词义：colour-coded 按颜色分类；practical matter 务实之举；boil 煮沸。

双版译文：
草稿：直到20世纪早期，儿童根本不被颜色编码：在家用洗衣机之前的时代，所有婴儿穿白色作为务实的事，因为让衣服干净的唯一方法是煮沸它们。
定稿：直到 20 世纪初，儿童才完全没有按颜色分类：在家用洗衣机问世之前的年代，所有婴儿都穿白色衣服，这是出于实用考虑，因为清洗衣服的唯一方法是煮沸。
7
What's more, both boys and girls wore what were thought of as gender-neutral dresses.
更重要的是，男孩和女孩都穿被视为性别中立的服装。
🔍 语法解析
宾语从句：wore what were thought of as gender-neutral dresses（what 引导宾语从句）。
词义：gender-neutral 性别中立的。

双版译文：
草稿：更重要的是，男孩和女孩都穿被认为是性别中立的衣服。
定稿：更重要的是，男孩和女孩都穿被视为性别中立的服装。
8
When nursery colours were introduced, pink was actually considered the more masculine colour, a pastel version of red, which was associated with strength. Blue, with its intimations of the Virgin Mary, constancy and faithfulness, symbolized femininity.
当婴幼儿颜色引入时，粉色实际上被认为是更男性化的颜色——红色的柔和版本，与力量相关联。而蓝色，因其令人联想到圣母玛利亚、忠贞和忠诚，象征着女性气质。
🔍 长难句拆解
第一句：When nursery colours were introduced（时间状语从句）+ pink was considered the more masculine colour（主句）+ a pastel version of red（同位语）+ which was associated with strength（非限制性定语从句）。
第二句：Blue ... symbolized femininity（主+谓+宾），with its intimations ... → 介词短语补充说明。
💡 Q27 考点：过去蓝色象征女性（femininity）——与今天相反！
词义：masculine 男性化的；pastel 柔和的（浅色）；intimation 暗示；femininity 女性气质。

双版译文：
草稿：当婴幼儿颜色被引入时，粉色实际上被认为更男性化的颜色，红色的柔和版本，与力量相关联。蓝色，带着它对圣母玛利亚、忠贞和忠诚的暗示，象征女性气质。
定稿：当婴幼儿颜色引入时，粉色实际上被认为是更男性化的颜色——红色的柔和版本，与力量相关联。而蓝色，因其令人联想到圣母玛利亚、忠贞和忠诚，象征着女性气质。
9
It was not until the mid-1980s, when amplifying age and sex differences became a dominant children's marketing strategy, that pink fully came into its own, when it began to seem inherently attractive to girls, part of what defined them as female, at least for the first few critical years.
直到 1980 年代中期——当时放大年龄和性别差异成为儿童营销的主导策略——粉色才完全崭露头角，开始对女孩显得天生具有吸引力，成为定义她们女性身份的一部分，至少在最初关键的几年里是如此。
🔍 长难句拆解
强调句型：It was not until ... that ...（直到……才……）
两个 when 从句：

同位语：part of what defined them as female。

词义：amplify 放大；dominant 主导的；come into its own 崭露头角；inherently 天生地。

双版译文：

26. By saying "it is ... the rainbow" (Line 2~3, Para. 1), the author means pink ______.
[A] should not be the sole representation of girlhood [B] should not be associated with girls' innocence [C] cannot explain girls' lack of imagination [D] cannot influence girls' lives and interests
参考答案：A

✅ 正确答案：A（should not be the sole representation of girlhood 粉色不应是少女时代的唯一代表）

定位：P1 · S02 — "it is such a tiny slice of the rainbow and, though it may celebrate girlhood in one way, it also ... fuses girls' identity to appearance"

推理：作者说粉色只是"彩虹中极小的一段"（a tiny slice of the rainbow）——彩虹代表多彩多样的女孩特质，而粉色只是其中之一，却被当成了女孩身份的全部代表。紧接着说它"把女孩身份与外表绑定"（fuses girls' identity to appearance），说明作者认为粉色不应该成为少女时代的唯一代表。

❌ B 错误：should not be associated with girls' innocence（不应与女孩的天真联系起来）——S03 说粉色把女孩呈现为"天真的证据"（evidence of innocence），作者是在批评这种过度绑定，但核心意思是"粉色不该是唯一代表"，不是完全否认天真与粉色的联系。

❌ C 错误：cannot explain girls' lack of imagination（不能解释女孩缺乏想象力）——S04 说的是作者对"女孩生活缺乏想象力"感到失望，是另一件事。

❌ D 错误：cannot influence girls' lives and interests（不能影响女孩的生活和兴趣）——与原文相反！S01 说粉色在女孩生活中"pervasive"（无处不在），说明它确实影响了女孩。

27. According to Paragraph 2, which of the following is true of colours?
[A] Colours are encoded in girls' DNA. [B] Blue used to be regarded as the colour for girls. [C] Pink used to be a neutral colour in symbolising genders. [D] White is preferred by babies.
参考答案：B

✅ 正确答案：B（Blue used to be regarded as the colour for girls 蓝色过去被视为女孩的颜色）

定位：P2 · S08 — "Blue, with its intimations of the Virgin Mary, constancy and faithfulness, symbolized femininity"

推理：S08 明确说蓝色象征女性气质（symbolized femininity）——即过去蓝色被视为女孩的颜色，这与今天的观念（粉色代表女孩）完全相反。B 选项是原文的直接对应。

❌ A 错误：Colours are encoded in girls' DNA（颜色编码在女孩 DNA 里）——S05 说女孩对粉色的喜爱 "may seem ... encoded in their DNA, but ... it is not"（似乎编码在 DNA 里，但并非如此）——A 正是作者否定的观点！

❌ C 错误：Pink used to be a neutral colour（粉色过去是中性色）——S08 说粉色过去被认为更男性化（masculine），不是中性（neutral）。

❌ D 错误：White is preferred by babies（白色被婴儿偏爱）——S06 说婴儿穿白色是"practical matter"（务实选择），因为白色耐煮洗，不是"偏爱"。

28. The author suggests that our perception of children's psychological development was much influenced by ______.
[A] the marketing of products for children [B] the observation of children's nature [C] researches into children's behaviour [D] studies of childhood consumption
参考答案：A

✅ 正确答案：A（the marketing of products for children 儿童产品的营销）

定位：P3 · S10-S12 — "how profoundly marketing trends dictated our perception of what is natural to kids, including our core beliefs about their psychological development" + "toddler ... was popularised as a marketing trick by clothing manufacturers"

推理：作者明确说"营销趋势支配了我们对孩子天性的认知，包括对其心理发展的核心信念"。而且用 "toddler"（学步儿童）这个所谓"发育阶段"其实是 1930 年代服装制造商的营销技巧为例证。A 选项 the marketing of products for children 是对 marketing trends / marketing trick 的概括。

❌ B 错误：observation of children's nature（观察儿童天性）——作者恰恰认为我们的认知不是来自观察，而是被营销扭曲的。

❌ C 错误：researches into children's behaviour（对儿童行为的研究）——S11 作者说"我曾以为 toddler 是专家研究后的成果：错了"（wrong），C 正是被否定的假设。

❌ D 错误：studies of childhood consumption（童年消费研究）——Daniel Cook 是研究童年消费的历史学家，但作者强调的是营销本身塑造认知，不是"对消费的研究"。

29. We may learn from Paragraph 4 that department stores were advised to ______.
[A] focus on infant wear and older kids' clothes [B] attach equal importance to different genders [C] classify consumers into smaller groups [D] create some common shoppers' terms
参考答案：C

✅ 正确答案：C（classify consumers into smaller groups 把消费者分成更小的群体）

定位：P4 · S13-S15 — "they should create a 'third stepping stone' between infant wear and older kids' clothes" + "Splitting kids ... into ever-tinier categories has proved a sure-fire way to boost profits"

推理：行业刊物建议百货商店在婴儿装和大童装之间创造"第三块垫脚石"（即 toddler 装）——本质上就是把消费群体细分得更小（ever-tinier categories）。C 选项 classify consumers into smaller groups 是对此的准确概括。

❌ A 错误：focus on infant wear and older kids' clothes（聚焦婴儿装和大童装）——恰恰相反，建议是在两者之间创造新的中间类别。

❌ B 错误：attach equal importance to different genders（同等重视不同性别）——文中说的是放大性别差异（magnify gender differences），不是同等重视。

❌ D 错误：create some common shoppers' terms（创造常用购物术语）——"toddler"成为常用词是细分市场的结果/手段，不是建议本身的目标；建议的核心是创造中间类别来细分市场。

30. It can be concluded that girls' attraction to pink seems to be ______.
[A] clearly explained by their inborn tendency [B] fully understood by clothing manufacturers [C] mainly imposed by profit-driven businessmen [D] well interpreted by psychological experts
参考答案：C

✅ 正确答案：C（mainly imposed by profit-driven businessmen 主要是被逐利的商人强加的）

定位：P2 · S09 "pink fully came into its own, when it began to seem inherently attractive to girls"（1980 年代营销策略让它显得天生吸引女孩）+ P4 · S15 "Splitting kids ... into ever-tinier categories has proved a sure-fire way to boost profits" + "magnify gender differences—or invent them where they did not previously exist"

推理：全文的结论是：女孩对粉色的喜爱不是天生的，而是 1980 年代营销策略（放大性别差异、细分市场）为了提高利润（boost profits）而制造/强加的。C 选项 profit-driven businessmen（逐利的商人）准确概括了"营销/商人/利润"这三个关键词。

❌ A 错误：clearly explained by their inborn tendency（由天生倾向清楚解释）——与全文主旨相反！作者明确否定"天生论"（it is not / wrong）。

❌ B 错误：fully understood by clothing manufacturers（被服装制造商完全理解）——这是事实（制造商确实利用它），但不是"结论"的重点；且"fully understood"表述过强。

❌ D 错误：well interpreted by psychological experts（被心理学专家很好地解释）——S11 明确说作者曾以为专家研究解释了它："wrong"（错了）。
