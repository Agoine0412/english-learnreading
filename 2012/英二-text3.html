基因专利之争2012 考研英语（二）· Text 3

In 2010, a federal judge shook America's biotech industry to its core — the battle over gene patents

难度 考研级词汇·语法·结构 主题 · 生物科技长难句 5
Paragraph 1 — 法官裁决震动生物科技行业
1
In 2010, a federal judge shook America's biotech industry to its core.
2010 年，一位联邦法官从根本上撼动了美国生物科技行业。
🔍 语法解析
主干：a federal judge（主）+ shook（谓）+ America's biotech industry（宾）+ to its core（程度状语）。
词义：biotech 生物科技（biotechnology 缩写）；to its core 从根本上。
2
Companies had won patents for isolated DNA for decades—by 2005 some 20% of human genes were patented.
几十年来，公司一直为分离的 DNA 赢得专利——到 2005 年，约 20% 的人类基因已被授予专利。
🔍 语法解析
主干：Companies（主）+ had won（过去完成谓）+ patents（宾）。
破折号后补充：by 2005 some 20% of human genes were patented（被动语态）。
词义：isolated DNA 分离的 DNA；patent 专利（n./v.）。
3
But in March 2010 a judge ruled that genes were unpatentable. Executives were violently agitated.
但在 2010 年 3 月，一名法官裁定基因不可授予专利。高管们极为激动。
🔍 语法解析
第一句：a judge ruled that ...（主+谓+宾语从句）。
第二句：Executives were violently agitated（主+系+表）。
💡 Q31 考点：高管们极度不安，说明生物科技公司希望基因可以被专利。
词义：unpatentable 不可申请专利的；violently 激烈地；agitated 激动的/不安的。
4
The Biotechnology Industry Organisation (BIO), a trade group, assured members that this was just a "preliminary step" in a longer battle.
生物科技行业组织（BIO）——一个贸易团体——向成员保证，这只是更漫长斗争中的"初步一步"。
🔍 语法解析
主干：The BIO（主）+ assured（谓）+ members（间接宾语）+ that ...（宾语从句）。
同位语：a trade group 解释 BIO。
词义：assure 向……保证；preliminary 初步的。
Paragraph 2 — 上诉法院推翻原判
5
On July 29th they were relieved, at least temporarily.
7 月 29 日，他们松了一口气，至少暂时如此。
🔍 语法解析
主干：they were relieved（主+系+表）。
插入语：at least temporarily 表程度的限定。
词义：relieved 松一口气的；temporarily 暂时地。
6
A federal appeals court overturned the prior decision, ruling that Myriad Genetics could indeed hold patents to two genes that help forecast a woman's risk of breast cancer.
联邦上诉法院推翻了先前的裁决，裁定 Myriad Genetics 公司确实可以持有两项基因的专利，这两项基因有助于预测女性患乳腺癌的风险。
🔍 长难句拆解
主干：A federal appeals court（主）+ overturned（谓）+ the prior decision（宾）。
伴随状语：ruling that Myriad Genetics could indeed hold patents to two genes（现在分词短语作伴随状语）。
定语从句：that help forecast a woman's risk of breast cancer → 修饰 two genes。
词义：appeals court 上诉法院；overturn 推翻；forecast 预测；breast cancer 乳腺癌。
7
The chief executive of Myriad, a company in Utah, said the ruling was a blessing to firms and patients alike.
犹他州公司 Myriad 的首席执行官说，这项裁决对公司和患者都是福音。
🔍 语法解析
主干：The chief executive of Myriad（主）+ said（谓）+ （that）the ruling was a blessing（宾语从句，省略 that）。
同位语：a company in Utah 解释 Myriad。
词义：blessing 福音/好事；alike 同样地。
Paragraph 3 — 批评者的反对理由
8
But as companies continue their attempts at personalised medicine, the courts will remain rather busy.
但随着公司继续推进个性化医疗的尝试，法院将仍然相当繁忙。
🔍 语法解析
时间状语从句：as companies continue their attempts at personalised medicine（as = 随着）。
主句：the courts will remain rather busy（主+系+表）。
词义：personalised medicine 个性化医疗。
9
The Myriad case itself is probably not over. Critics make three main arguments against gene patents: a gene is a product of nature, so it may not be patented; gene patents suppress innovation rather than reward it; and patents' monopolies restrict access to genetic tests such as Myriad's.
Myriad 案本身可能还没有结束。批评者提出了反对基因专利的三个主要论点：基因是自然产物，因此不可授予专利；基因专利抑制而非奖励创新；专利垄断限制了对基因检测（如 Myriad 的检测）的获取。
🔍 长难句拆解
第一句：The Myriad case is probably not over（主+系+表）。
第二句：Critics make three main arguments against gene patents（主+谓+宾）+ 冒号引出三个并列分句：
  ① a gene is a product of nature, so it may not be patented
  ② gene patents suppress innovation rather than reward it
  ③ patents' monopolies restrict access to genetic tests such as Myriad's
💡 Q32 考点：批评者认为自然产物（如基因）不可专利——只有人造产品才能专利。
词义：suppress 抑制；monopoly 垄断；restrict access to 限制获取。
10
A growing number seem to agree. Last year a federal task-force urged reform for patents related to genetic tests. In October the Department of Justice filed a brief in the Myriad case, arguing that an isolated DNA molecule "is no less a product of nature...than are cotton fibres that have been separated from cotton seeds."
越来越多的人似乎认同这一点。去年，一个联邦特别工作组敦促对基因检测相关专利进行改革。10 月，司法部在 Myriad 案中提交了一份意见书，辩称分离的 DNA 分子"与从棉籽中分离出的棉纤维一样，同样是自然产物"。
🔍 长难句拆解
第一句：A growing number seem to agree（主+系+不定式表语）。
第二句：a federal task-force urged reform for patents related to genetic tests。
第三句：the Department of Justice filed a brief ... arguing that ...（现在分词作伴随状语）。
从句内比较："is no less a product of nature ... than are cotton fibres that have been separated from cotton seeds" → no less ... than（与……一样）；than are cotton fibres 为倒装结构；that have been separated → 定语从句修饰 fibres。
词义：task-force 特别工作组；file a brief 提交意见书；molecule 分子。
Paragraph 4 — 悬而未决的大问题
11
Despite the appeals court's decision, big questions remain unanswered. For example, it is unclear whether the sequencing of a whole genome violates the patents of individual genes within it.
尽管上诉法院作出了裁决，但重大问题仍未得到解答。例如，尚不清楚对整个人类基因组的测序是否侵犯了其中单个基因的专利。
🔍 语法解析
第一句：big questions remain unanswered（主+系+表）。
第二句：it is unclear whether ...（it 形式主语 + whether 主语从句）。
词义：sequencing 测序；genome 基因组；violate 侵犯/违反。
12
The case may yet reach the Supreme Court.
此案可能还会上诉至最高法院。
🔍 语法解析
主干：The case（主）+ may yet reach（谓）+ the Supreme Court（宾）。
词义：Supreme Court 最高法院。
Paragraph 5 — 行业的新动向
13
As the industry advances, however, other suits may have an even greater impact. Companies are unlikely to file many more patents for human DNA molecules—most are already patented or in the public domain.
然而，随着行业发展，其他诉讼可能产生更大的影响。公司不太可能为人类 DNA 分子申请更多专利——大多数已经获得专利或进入公共领域。
🔍 语法解析
第一句：As the industry advances（时间状语从句）+ other suits may have an even greater impact。
第二句：Companies are unlikely to file many more patents for human DNA molecules + 破折号后解释（most are already patented or in the public domain）。
词义：file patents 申请专利；public domain 公共领域（不受专利保护）。
14
Firms are now studying how genes interact, looking for correlations that might be used to determine the causes of disease or predict a drug's efficacy, companies are eager to win patents for "connecting the dots", explains Hans Sauer, a lawyer for the BIO.
公司现在正在研究基因如何相互作用，寻找可能用于确定疾病原因或预测药物疗效的相关性；BIO 的律师 Hans Sauer 解释说，公司渴望赢得"串联线索"的专利。
🔍 长难句拆解
主干：Firms are now studying how genes interact（主+谓+宾语从句），looking for correlations ...（现在分词作伴随状语）。
破折号后：companies are eager to win patents for "connecting the dots", explains Hans Sauer（引述结构）。
💡 Q33 考点：公司渴望赢得"connecting the dots"（串联线索）的专利 = 建立疾病相关性（disease correlations）。
词义：interact 相互作用；correlation 相关性；efficacy 药效；connect the dots 串联线索（喻指发现基因间的联系）。
Paragraph 6 — 即将到来的重大诉讼
15
Their success may be determined by a suit related to this issue, brought by the Mayo Clinic, which the Supreme Court will hear in its next term.
他们的成功可能取决于 Mayo 诊所提起的一起与此问题相关的诉讼，最高法院将在下一开庭期审理此案。
🔍 长难句拆解
主干：Their success may be determined by a suit（主+被动谓）。
过去分词后置定语：brought by the Mayo Clinic 修饰 a suit。
非限制性定语从句：which the Supreme Court will hear in its next term 修饰 a suit。
词义：determine 决定；term （法庭）开庭期。
16
The BIO recently held a convention which included sessions to coach lawyers on the shifting landscape for patents. Each meeting was packed.
BIO 最近举办了一次大会，其中包括为律师提供关于专利格局变化的培训课程。每场会议都座无虚席。
🔍 长难句拆解
第一句：The BIO held a convention（主+谓+宾）+ which included sessions to coach lawyers on the shifting landscape for patents（定语从句修饰 convention）。
第二句：Each meeting was packed（主+系+表）。
💡 Q34 考点：会议座无虚席 → 基因专利问题广受关注（a great concern）。
词义：convention 大会；coach 培训；shifting landscape 不断变化的格局；packed 座无虚席的。

📝 题目答案速查 ▼ 展开

31. It can be learned from Paragraph 1 that the biotech companies would like ______.
[A] their executives to be active [B] judges to rule out gene patenting [C] genes to be patentable [D] the BIO to issue a warning
参考答案：C

✅ 正确答案：C（genes to be patentable 基因可以被专利）

定位：P1 · S02-S04 — "Companies had won patents for isolated DNA for decades"（公司几十年一直赢取基因专利）+ "a judge ruled that genes were unpatentable"（法官裁定基因不可专利）+ "Executives were violently agitated"（高管们极度不安）

推理：公司几十年来一直在为基因申请专利并取得成功，当法官裁定"基因不可专利"时高管们极度不安（violently agitated）——这说明公司希望基因继续可以被专利（patentable）。C 选项直接对应。

❌ A 错误：their executives to be active（希望高管积极）——executives 是"因裁决而激动"的主体，不是公司希望的对象。

❌ B 错误：judges to rule out gene patenting（希望法官排除基因专利）——恰恰相反！公司不希望排除基因专利，而是希望保留它。

❌ D 错误：the BIO to issue a warning（希望 BIO 发出警告）——S04 说 BIO 主动"assured members"（向成员保证），不是公司希望它警告。

32. Those who are against gene patents believe that ______.
[A] genetic tests are not reliable [B] only man-made products are patentable [C] patents on genes depend much on innovation [D] courts should restrict access to genetic tests
参考答案：B

✅ 正确答案：B（only man-made products are patentable 只有人造产品才能专利）

定位：P3 · S09 — 批评者第一个论点："a gene is a product of nature, so it may not be patented"（基因是自然产物，因此不可专利）

推理：批评者的核心逻辑是"自然产物不可专利"——言下之意：只有人造的（man-made）产品才可专利。基因是自然产物，所以不可专利。B 选项是对这一逻辑的反向概括。

❌ A 错误：genetic tests are not reliable（基因检测不可靠）——文中未说检测不可靠，只是说专利垄断"restrict access to"（限制获取）检测。

❌ C 错误：patents on genes depend much on innovation（基因专利很大程度上依赖创新）——批评者恰恰说基因专利"suppress innovation rather than reward it"（抑制而非奖励创新），与 C 相反。

❌ D 错误：courts should restrict access to genetic tests（法院应限制检测获取）——S09 说的是专利垄断（monopolies）在限制获取，批评者反对这种限制，不是主张限制。

33. According to Hans Sauer, companies are eager to win patents for ______.
[A] establishing disease correlations [B] discovering gene interactions [C] drawing pictures of genes [D] identifying human DNA
参考答案：B

✅ 正确答案：B（discovering gene interactions 发现基因相互作用）

定位：P5 · S14 — "Firms are now studying how genes interact, looking for correlations ... companies are eager to win patents for 'connecting the dots'"

推理：Hans Sauer 说公司"正在研究基因如何相互作用（genes interact）……渴望赢得'串联线索'（connecting the dots）的专利"。"connecting the dots" 正是指发现基因之间的相互联系/相互作用。B 选项 discovering gene interactions 是对此最直接的概括。

❌ A 错误：establishing disease correlations（建立疾病相关性）——looking for correlations 是研究方法/目的之一，但 Sauer 说的"connecting the dots"核心是基因相互作用，相关性是寻找的结果。

❌ C 错误：drawing pictures of genes（绘制基因图谱）——"dots"（点）是比喻，不是真的画图。

❌ D 错误：identifying human DNA（识别人类 DNA）——这是过去的事（P5 S13 说 DNA 专利已饱和），不是现在渴望的方向。

34. By saying "Each meeting was packed" (line 4, Para. 6) the author means that ______.
[A] the supreme court was authoritative [B] the BIO was a powerful organisation [C] gene patenting was a great concern [D] lawyers were keen to attend conventions
参考答案：C

✅ 正确答案：C（gene patenting was a great concern 基因专利问题广受关注）

定位：P6 · S16 — "The BIO recently held a convention which included sessions to coach lawyers on the shifting landscape for patents. Each meeting was packed."

推理：BIO 举办的专利培训会"每场都座无虚席（packed）"——那么多律师挤着去听基因专利的培训，说明基因专利是大家高度关注的问题（a great concern）。作者用"座无虚席"的细节来强调该问题的重要性。

❌ A 错误：the supreme court was authoritative（最高法院有权威）——这是 S15 的背景，不是"packed"要表达的意思。

❌ B 错误：the BIO was a powerful organisation（BIO 是强大组织）——"packed"形容的是会议出席情况，不是组织的权力。

❌ D 错误：lawyers were keen to attend conventions（律师热衷于参加会议）——虽然律师确实去了，但"packed"强调的是议题受关注，而非律师爱开会。

35. Generally speaking, the author's attitude toward gene patenting is ______.
[A] critical [B] supportive [C] scornful [D] objective
参考答案：D

✅ 正确答案：D（objective 客观的）

定位：全文叙事方式——作者以中性、客观的口吻陈述事实：P1 公司赢得专利（客观陈述）、P2 上诉法院推翻原判（客观陈述）、P3 批评者的三大理由（转述他人观点，用 "critics make ... arguments"）、P4-P6 未决问题和行业动向（客观报道）。

推理：作者通篇都在呈现双方观点——既写了公司对专利的渴望（S02-S03），也写了批评者的反对理由（S09），但没有明确表达自己的立场。这种"两边都讲、不表态"的写法是典型的客观（objective）态度。

❌ A 错误：critical（批评的）——作者虽然报道了批评者的观点，但那是"转述"（critics make arguments），不是作者自己的立场。

❌ B 错误：supportive（支持的）——同样，作者没有表达对基因专利的支持。

❌ C 错误：scornful（轻蔑的）——全文没有轻蔑的语气。
