外部董事的困境2011 考研英语（二）· Text 1

Ruth Simmons joined Goldman Sachs's board as an outside director — outside directors' role, risks, and incentives

难度 考研级词汇·语法·结构 主题 · 公司治理长难句 5
Paragraph 1 — Ruth Simmons 的案例
1
Ruth Simmons joined Goldman Sachs's board as an outside director in January 2000; a year later she became president of Brown University.
2000 年 1 月，Ruth Simmons 以外部董事的身份加入高盛董事会；一年后，她成为布朗大学的校长。
🔍 语法解析
并列句：分号连接两个独立分句，讲述 Simmons 的两个身份转变。
  前分句：Ruth Simmons（主）+ joined（谓）+ Goldman Sachs's board（宾）+ as an outside director（方式状语）
  后分句：a year later（时间状语）+ she（主）+ became（系）+ president（表）
词义：outside director 外部董事（不在公司任职、只参与董事会的独立人士）。
2
For the rest of the decade she apparently managed both roles without attracting much criticism.
在那十年的剩余时间里，她显然同时兼顾两个角色，没有招致太多批评。
🔍 语法解析
主干：she（主）+ managed（谓）+ both roles（宾）+ without attracting much criticism（方式状语，介词短语）。
词义：apparently 显然/表面上看；criticism 批评。
3
But by the end of 2009, Ms. Simmons was under fire for having sat on Goldman's compensation committee; how could she have let those enormous bonus payouts pass unremarked?
但到 2009 年底，Simmons 女士因在高盛薪酬委员会任职而受到抨击：她怎能任由那些巨额奖金支出悄无声息地通过呢？
🔍 长难句拆解
主句：Ms. Simmons was under fire for having sat on Goldman's compensation committee（主+系+表 + for 原因状语）。
分号后反问：how could she have let those enormous bonus payouts pass unremarked? → 反问句，用 could have let 表"本该阻止却未阻止"的责备。
💡 Q21 考点：Simmons 被批评的原因是她任由巨额奖金通过（未能履行职责）。
词义：under fire 受到抨击；compensation committee 薪酬委员会；unremarked 未被注意的。
4
By February the next year, Ms. Simmons had left the board.
到第二年 2 月，Simmons 女士已经离开了董事会。
🔍 语法解析
时态：had left 过去完成时，强调"到 2010 年 2 月这个时间点之前已经离开"。
词义：board 董事会。
5
The position was just taking up too much time, she said.
她说，这个职位占用了太多时间。
🔍 语法解析
引述结构：she said 置于句末。
词义：take up 占用（时间/空间）。
Paragraph 2 — 外部董事应有的角色
6
Outside directors are supposed to serve as helpful, yet less biased, advisers on a firm's board.
外部董事本应在公司董事会中充当有益的、然而偏见较少的顾问。
🔍 语法解析
主干：Outside directors（主）+ are supposed to serve（谓）+ as ... advisers（主补）。
修饰：helpful, yet less biased → 两个并列形容词修饰 advisers，yet 表"然而"。
词义：be supposed to 应该/本应；biased 有偏见的。
7
Having made their wealth and their reputations elsewhere, they presumably have enough independence to disagree with the chief executive's proposals.
由于在其他地方已经积累了财富和声誉，他们大概有足够的独立性去反对首席执行官的建议。
🔍 长难句拆解
主干：they（主）+ have（谓）+ enough independence（宾）+ to disagree with ...（不定式后置定语）。
分词状语：Having made their wealth and their reputations elsewhere → 现在分词完成式作原因状语（"因为已经……所以……"）。
词义：elsewhere 在别处；presumably 大概/推测起来；chief executive 首席执行官（CEO）。
8
If the sky, and the share price, is falling, outside directors should be able to give advice based on having weathered their own crises.
如果天塌下来——股价下跌——外部董事应该能够基于自己经历过危机的经验提供建议。
🔍 语法解析
条件状语从句：If the sky, and the share price, is falling（插入语 and the share price 将 sky 和股价并列）。
主句：outside directors should be able to give advice based on having weathered their own crises（过去分词短语 based on ... 作后置定语修饰 advice）。
词义：weather 经受住/渡过（危机）；share price 股价。
Paragraph 3 — 俄亥俄大学的研究：外部董事突然离职的后果
9
The researchers from Ohio University used a database that covered more than 10,000 firms and more than 64,000 different directors between 1989 and 2004.
俄亥俄大学的研究人员使用了一个数据库，覆盖了 1989 年至 2004 年间超过 10000 家公司和 64000 多位不同的董事。
🔍 语法解析
主干：The researchers（主）+ used（谓）+ a database（宾）。
定语从句：that covered more than 10,000 firms and more than 64,000 different directors between 1989 and 2004 → 修饰 database。
词义：database 数据库；firm 公司。
10
Then they simply checked which directors stayed from one proxy statement to the next.
然后他们只是核对哪些董事从一份委托书持续到下一份。
🔍 语法解析
主干：they checked（主+谓）+ which directors stayed（宾语从句）。
词义：proxy statement 股东委托书（proxy 代理/委托）。
11
The most likely reason for departing a board was age, so the researchers concentrated on those "surprise" disappearances by directors under the age of 70.
离开董事会最可能的原因是年龄，因此研究人员聚焦于 70 岁以下董事的"突然"消失。
🔍 语法解析
因果并列句：so 连接结果。
  前分句：The most likely reason ... was age（主+系+表）
  后分句：the researchers concentrated on those "surprise" disappearances by directors under the age of 70
词义：concentrate on 聚焦于；under the age of 70 70 岁以下。
12
They found that after a surprise departure, the probability that the company will subsequently have to restate earnings increases by nearly 20%.
他们发现，在突然离职之后，公司随后不得不重述收益的概率增加了近 20%。
🔍 长难句拆解
主干：They found that ...（主+谓+宾语从句）。
宾语从句：the probability ... increases by nearly 20%。
  ① after a surprise departure → 时间状语
  ② that the company will subsequently have to restate earnings → 同位语从句修饰 probability
💡 语法点：probability 后接 that 同位语从句，解释"这种概率的具体内容"。
词义：restate 重述（收益）；subsequently 随后。
13
The likelihood of being named in a federal class-action lawsuit also increases, and the stock is likely to perform worse.
被列入联邦集体诉讼的可能性也会增加，股票表现可能更差。
🔍 语法解析
并列句：and 连接两个分句。
  前分句：The likelihood of being named in a federal class-action lawsuit increases
  后分句：the stock is likely to perform worse
词义：likelihood 可能性；class-action lawsuit 集体诉讼；perform worse 表现更差。
14
The effect tended to be larger for larger firms.
对于较大的公司，这种影响往往更大。
🔍 语法解析
主干：The effect（主）+ tended to be（谓）+ larger（表）。
词义：tend to 倾向于/往往。
15
Although a correlation between them leaving and subsequent bad performance at the firm is suggestive, it does not mean that such directors are always jumping off a sinking ship.
尽管他们的离开与公司随后表现不佳之间的相关性具有启发性，但这并不意味着这些董事总是在跳离一艘正在下沉的船。
🔍 长难句拆解
让步状语从句：Although a correlation between them leaving and subsequent bad performance at the firm is suggestive。
  them leaving → 动名词复合结构（逻辑主语 them + leaving）
主句：it does not mean that such directors are always jumping off a sinking ship（宾语从句）。
💡 习语：jump off a sinking ship = 弃船逃生/离开注定失败的事业。
词义：correlation 相关性；suggestive 有启发性的/暗示性的。
16
Often they "trade up," leaving riskier, smaller firms for larger and more stable firms.
他们常常"向上交易"，离开风险较高、较小的公司，转向更大更稳定的公司。
🔍 语法解析
主干：they "trade up"（主+谓）。
伴随状语：leaving riskier, smaller firms for larger and more stable firms → 现在分词短语，说明 trade up 的具体动作。
词义：trade up 向上换/升级（跳槽到更好的公司）；stable 稳定的。
Paragraph 4 — 如何留住外部董事
17
But the researchers believe that outside directors have an easier time of avoiding a blow to their reputations if they leave a firm before bad news breaks, even if a review of history shows they were on the board at the time any wrongdoing occurred.
但研究人员认为，如果外部董事在坏消息爆出之前离开公司，他们就能更容易地避免声誉受损，即使历史回顾显示他们在任何不当行为发生时仍在董事会任职。
🔍 长难句拆解
主干：the researchers believe that ...（主+谓+宾语从句）。
宾语从句：outside directors have an easier time of avoiding a blow to their reputations。
  ① if they leave a firm before bad news breaks → 条件状语从句
  ② even if a review of history shows they were on the board at the time any wrongdoing occurred → 让步状语从句，内含宾语从句 shows（that）they were ... 和定语从句 any wrongdoing occurred（省略 when）
💡 Q24 考点：此句说明外部董事倾向于在坏消息前离开以保声誉。
词义：a blow to 对……的打击；wrongdoing 不当行为。
18
Firms who want to keep their outside directors through tough times may have to create incentives.
想在艰难时期留住外部董事的公司可能不得不创造激励措施。
🔍 语法解析
主干：Firms ... may have to create incentives（主+谓+宾）。
定语从句：who want to keep their outside directors through tough times → 修饰 Firms。
💡 Q24 考点：公司需要创造 incentives（激励）来留住外部董事。
词义：incentive 激励/诱因。
19
Otherwise outside directors will follow the example of Ms. Simmons, once again very popular on campus.
否则外部董事将会效仿 Simmons 女士的例子——她再一次在校园里非常受欢迎。
🔍 语法解析
主干：outside directors will follow the example of Ms. Simmons（主+谓+宾）。
独立主格/补充说明：once again very popular on campus → 补充说明 Simmons 离开董事会后回到大学重新受欢迎。
词义：otherwise 否则；follow the example of 效仿。

📝 题目答案速查 ▼ 展开

21. According to Paragraph 1, Ms. Simmons was criticized for ______.
[A] gaining excessive profits [B] failing to fulfill her duty [C] refusing to make compromises [D] leaving the board in tough times
参考答案：B

✅ 正确答案：B（failing to fulfill her duty 未能履行职责）

定位：P1 · S03 — "Ms. Simmons was under fire for having sat on Goldman's compensation committee; how could she have let those enormous bonus payouts pass unremarked?"

推理：她被批评的核心是"坐在薪酬委员会上却任由巨额奖金通过（pass unremarked）"——作为薪酬委员会成员，她有责任质疑/监督奖金发放，但她没有尽到这个职责。B 选项 failing to fulfill her duty（未履行职责）正是对此的概括。

❌ A 错误：gaining excessive profits（获取超额利润）——被批评的不是她自己获利，而是她没阻止高盛的巨额奖金。她本人没有 gain profits。

❌ C 错误：refusing to make compromises（拒绝妥协）——文中未提妥协问题。

❌ D 错误：leaving the board in tough times（在困难时期离开董事会）——离开董事会是她被批评后的结果，不是她被批评的原因。

22. We learn from Paragraph 2 that outside directors are supposed to be ______.
[A] generous investors [B] unbiased executives [C] share price forecasters [D] independent advisers
参考答案：D

✅ 正确答案：D（independent advisers 独立的顾问）

定位：P2 · S06-S07 — "serve as helpful, yet less biased, advisers" + "they presumably have enough independence to disagree with the chief executive's proposals"

推理：S06 说外部董事应该是 advisers（顾问），S07 说他们要有 independence（独立性）来反对 CEO。合起来 = independent advisers（独立顾问）。D 选项是两句话的综合概括。

❌ A 错误：generous investors（慷慨的投资者）——文中未提他们投资。

❌ B 错误：unbiased executives（无偏见的高管）——executives（高管）不准确，他们是董事（directors），且核心是"独立"而非只是"无偏见"。

❌ C 错误：share price forecasters（股价预测者）——S08 提到股价，但只是举例"股价下跌时"的场景，不是说他们预测股价。

23. According to the researchers from Ohio University, after an outside director's surprise departure, the firm is likely to ______.
[A] become more stable [B] report increased earnings [C] do less well in the stock market [D] perform worse in lawsuits
参考答案：C

✅ 正确答案：C（do less well in the stock market 在股市中表现更差）

定位：P3 · S12-S13 — "the probability that the company will ... have to restate earnings increases" + "the stock is likely to perform worse"

推理：S13 明确说"the stock is likely to perform worse"（股票表现可能更差）。C 选项 do less well in the stock market 正是 stock perform worse 的同义转述。

❌ A 错误：become more stable（变得更稳定）——与文意相反，公司变得不稳定。

❌ B 错误：report increased earnings（报告收益增加）——与文意相反，S12 说重述收益的概率增加（通常是下调收益）。

❌ D 错误：perform worse in lawsuits（在诉讼中表现更差）——S13 说的是"被列入集体诉讼的可能性增加"（likelihood of being named increases），不是"在诉讼中表现更差"。

24. It can be inferred from the last paragraph that outside directors ______.
[A] may stay for the attractive offers from the firm [B] have often had records of wrongdoings in the firm [C] are accustomed to stress-free work in the firm [D] will decline incentives from the firm
参考答案：A

✅ 正确答案：A（may stay for the attractive offers from the firm 可能因公司提供的诱人条件而留下）

定位：P4 · S18 — "Firms who want to keep their outside directors through tough times may have to create incentives"

推理：公司必须创造 incentives（激励/诱人条件）来留住外部董事 → 反推可知：外部董事可能会因为这些诱人条件而留下（stay for the attractive offers）。A 是合理的推断。

❌ B 错误：have often had records of wrongdoings（经常有不当行为记录）——S17 说"即使历史显示他们在不当行为发生时在董事会"，但这是说"在场"而非"有不当行为记录"。

❌ C 错误：are accustomed to stress-free work（习惯了无压力的工作）——文中未提工作压力大小。

❌ D 错误：will decline incentives（会拒绝激励）——与文意相反！公司正是靠 incentives 来吸引他们留下，推断应是可以被激励留住，而非拒绝。

25. The author's attitude toward the role of outside directors is ______.
[A] permissive [B] positive [C] scornful [D] critical
参考答案：B

✅ 正确答案：B（positive 肯定的）

定位：P2 对外部董事角色的描述 — "helpful, yet less biased, advisers"（有益且偏见较少的顾问）+ "have enough independence to disagree"（有足够独立性提出异议）+ "should be able to give advice based on having weathered their own crises"（应能基于自身危机经验提供建议）

推理：作者用 helpful（有益的）、less biased（较少偏见的）、should be able to give advice（应能提供建议）等词汇描述外部董事，整体是肯定/正面的态度。P3-P4 的研究虽然指出外部董事离职带来的问题，但对外部董事角色本身的定位是积极的。

❌ A 错误：permissive（放任的）——permissive 强调"宽容放纵"，不是对角色价值的肯定。

❌ C 错误：scornful（轻蔑的）——作者没有轻蔑外部董事，反而肯定其价值。这是你当初误选的选项。

❌ D 错误：critical（批评的）——虽然文章讨论了外部董事的问题（如离职影响），但整体态度不是批评外部董事角色本身。
