报业危机与转型2011 考研英语（二）· Text 2

Whatever happened to the death of newspaper? — the newspaper industry's crisis and comeback

难度 考研级词汇·语法·结构 主题 · 传媒产业长难句 4
Paragraph 1 — 报纸会消亡吗？
1
Whatever happened to the death of newspaper?
报纸的死亡到底怎么了？
🔍 语法解析
反问句：作者用"报纸的死亡到底怎么了？"开篇，暗示报纸并没有如预期般死亡。这个设问引出全文"报纸挺过了危机"的主题。
词义：whatever happened to 到底发生了什么/怎么样了（口语化疑问）。
2
A year ago the end seemed near.
一年前，末日似乎临近了。
🔍 语法解析
主干：the end（主）+ seemed（系）+ near（表）。
词义：the end 此处指报纸的末日。
3
The recession threatened to remove the advertising and readers that had not already fled to the internet.
经济衰退威胁着要带走那些尚未逃往互联网的广告和读者。
🔍 语法解析
主干：The recession（主）+ threatened（谓）+ to remove the advertising and readers（不定式宾）。
定语从句：that had not already fled to the internet → 修饰 the advertising and readers。
词义：recession 经济衰退；flee to 逃往。
4
Newspapers like the San Francisco Chronicle were chronicling their own doom.
像《旧金山纪事报》这样的报纸正在记录自己的厄运。
🔍 语法解析
主干：Newspapers（主）+ were chronicling（过去进行时谓）+ their own doom（宾）。
💡 双关：chronicle 既是"记录"（报纸的本职工作），又暗示报纸在"记录自己走向灭亡的过程"。
词义：chronicle 记录（编年史式记载）；doom 厄运/灭亡。
5
America's Federal Trade Commission launched a round of talks about how to save newspapers.
美国联邦贸易委员会发起了一轮关于如何拯救报纸的讨论。
🔍 语法解析
主干：America's Federal Trade Commission（主）+ launched（谓）+ a round of talks（宾）。
修饰：about how to save newspapers → 介词短语 + 疑问词+不定式结构。
词义：launch 发起；a round of talks 一轮会谈。
6
Should they become charitable corporations? Should the state subsidize them? It will hold another meeting soon. But the discussions now seem out of date.
它们应该变成慈善公司吗？国家应该补贴它们吗？（委员会）很快就会再开一次会。但这些讨论现在看来已经过时了。
🔍 语法解析
一般疑问句：Should they become ...? 连续两个反问，列举拯救方案的讨论。
转折：But the discussions now seem out of date → 为下文的"报纸其实挺过来了"做铺垫。
词义：charitable corporation 慈善公司；subsidize 补贴；out of date 过时的。
Paragraph 2 — 报纸其实挺过了危机
7
In much of the world there is little sign of crisis.
在世界大部分地区，几乎没有危机的迹象。
🔍 语法解析
there be 句型：there is little sign of crisis。little 表示"几乎没有"（否定含义）。
8
German and Brazilian papers have shrugged off the recession.
德国和巴西的报纸已经摆脱了衰退的影响。
🔍 语法解析
主干：German and Brazilian papers（主）+ have shrugged off（现在完成谓）+ the recession（宾）。
词义：shrug off 摆脱/不屑一顾（耸肩甩掉，比喻不受影响）。
9
Even American newspapers, which inhabit the most troubled corner of the global industry, have not only survived but often returned to profit.
即使是美国报纸——它们身处全球报业最麻烦的角落——不仅生存了下来，而且常常恢复了盈利。
🔍 长难句拆解
主干：Even American newspapers ... have not only survived but often returned to profit（not only ... but 并列结构）。
非限制性定语从句：which inhabit the most troubled corner of the global industry → 修饰 American newspapers。
词义：inhabit 居住于/身处；return to profit 恢复盈利。
10
Not the 20% profit margins that were routine a few years ago, but profit all the same.
不是几年前常见的 20% 利润率，但无论如何还是盈利的。
🔍 语法解析
省略句：完整形式为"Not the 20% profit margins ... but (they have returned to) profit all the same"。
定语从句：that were routine a few years ago → 修饰 margins。
词义：profit margin 利润率；all the same 无论如何/尽管如此。
Paragraph 3 — 报业为生存付出的代价
11
It has not been much fun.
这并不好玩。
🔍 语法解析
主干：It has not been much fun（主+系+表）。承上启下——虽然活下来了，但过程很痛苦。
12
Many papers stayed afloat by pushing journalists overboard.
许多报纸靠把记者"推下船"来维持生存。
🔍 语法解析
主干：Many papers（主）+ stayed afloat（谓）+ by pushing journalists overboard（方式状语）。
💡 暗喻：stay afloat（保持漂浮）vs push ... overboard（推下船）= 靠裁员来维持经营。
词义：stay afloat 维持运营/不沉没；push overboard 推下船/解雇。
13
The American Society of News Editors reckons that 13,500 newsroom jobs have gone since 2007.
美国新闻编辑协会估计，自 2007 年以来已有 13500 个编辑室职位消失。
🔍 语法解析
主干：The American Society of News Editors（主）+ reckons（谓）+ that ...（宾语从句）。
词义：reckon 估计；newsroom 编辑室/新闻编辑部。
14
Readers are paying more for slimmer products. Some papers even had the nerve to refuse delivery to distant suburbs.
读者花更多的钱买更薄的报纸。有些报纸甚至有胆量拒绝向遥远的郊区送报。
🔍 语法解析
第一句：Readers are paying more for slimmer products（主+谓+宾）。
第二句：Some papers had the nerve to refuse delivery to distant suburbs（had the nerve to = 有胆量做某事）。
词义：slim 薄的/缩水的；have the nerve to 有胆量/竟敢。
15
Yet these desperate measures have proved the right ones and, sadly for many journalists, they can be pushed further.
然而这些孤注一掷的措施被证明是正确的，而且——对许多记者来说很悲哀——它们还可以进一步推进。
🔍 长难句拆解
主干：these desperate measures have proved the right ones（主+谓+宾补）。
插入语：sadly for many journalists → 作者对记者处境的同情。
并列分句：they can be pushed further（被动语态）。
词义：desperate 孤注一掷的/绝望的；push further 进一步推进（此处指进一步裁员）。
Paragraph 4 — 报纸正在变得更平衡
16
Newspapers are becoming more balanced businesses, with a healthier mix of revenues from readers and advertisers.
报纸正变成更平衡的企业，来自读者和广告商的收入结构更健康。
🔍 语法解析
主干：Newspapers are becoming more balanced businesses（主+谓+表）。
伴随状语：with a healthier mix of revenues from readers and advertisers → 具体说明"更平衡"的含义。
💡 Q28 考点：balanced businesses = 收入来源更平衡（不单纯依赖广告）。
17
American papers have long been highly unusual in their reliance on ads. Fully 87% of their revenues came from advertising in 2008, according to the OECD. In Japan the proportion is 35%. Not surprisingly, Japanese newspapers are much more stable.
美国报纸长期以来在依赖广告方面极不寻常。据经合组织统计，2008 年它们 87% 的收入来自广告。而在日本这一比例是 35%。毫不奇怪，日本报纸要稳定得多。
🔍 语法解析
并列句组：四个短句构成对比论证——美国 87% vs 日本 35% → 日本更稳定。
插入语：according to the OECD → 交代数据来源。
词义：reliance 依赖；proportion 比例；OECD 经合组织。
Paragraph 5 — 报业裁掉了最不具特色的部分
18
The whirlwind that swept through newsrooms harmed everybody, but much of the damage has been concentrated in areas where newspapers are least distinctive.
席卷编辑室的旋风伤害了每个人，但大部分损害集中在报纸最缺乏特色的领域。
🔍 长难句拆解
主干：The whirlwind ... harmed everybody（主+谓+宾）。
定语从句：that swept through newsrooms → 修饰 whirlwind。
转折并列句：but much of the damage has been concentrated in areas where newspapers are least distinctive → where 引导定语从句修饰 areas。
词义：whirlwind 旋风（比喻裁员风暴）；distinctive 独特的/有特色的。
19
Car and film reviewers have gone. So have science and general business reporters. Foreign bureaus have been savagely cut off.
汽车和电影评论员消失了。科学和普通商业记者也消失了。国外分社被野蛮地砍掉。
🔍 语法解析
So 倒装：So have science and general business reporters = Science and general business reporters have also gone（so 位于句首，主谓倒装）。
被动语态：have been savagely cut off。
词义：reviewer 评论员；bureau 分社/办事处；savagely 野蛮地。
20
Newspapers are less complete as a result. But completeness is no longer a virtue in the newspaper business.
结果，报纸不那么完整了。但在报业，完整性不再是一种美德。
🔍 语法解析
转折：But 引出作者的观点——报纸虽然不完整了，但在当下的报业环境下，追求完整已不再是优势。
词义：completeness 完整性；virtue 美德/优点。

📋 全文脉络 ▲ 收起

① P1: 一年前报纸濒临死亡，FTC 讨论拯救方案——但现在这些讨论已过时。
② P2: 报纸挺过了危机——德国巴西无恙，连美国报纸也恢复盈利。
③ P3: 生存的代价——裁员 13500 人、读者多花钱买薄报、拒绝远郊送报。
④ P4: 报纸收入来源更平衡——美国 87% 依赖广告 vs 日本 35%，日本更稳定。
⑤ P5: 裁掉的都是最不具特色的内容（评论员、外派记者），完整性不再是美德。

📝 题目答案速查 ▼ 展开

26. By saying "Newspapers like the San Francisco Chronicle were chronicling their own doom" (Line 3, Para. 1), the author indicates that newspapers ______.
[A] neglected the sign of crisis [B] failed to get state subsidies [C] were not charitable corporations [D] were in a difficult situation
参考答案：D

✅ 正确答案：D（were in a difficult situation 处境艰难）

定位：P1 · S04 — "Newspapers like the San Francisco Chronicle were chronicling their own doom"，紧接着 S03 说 "The recession threatened to remove the advertising and readers"。

推理：chronicling their own doom = "记录自己的厄运/灭亡"。报纸在报道自己走向灭亡的过程，说明它们当时处境非常艰难（in a difficult situation）。这是作者用"记录厄运"来描述报纸的困境。

❌ A 错误：neglected the sign of crisis（忽视了危机迹象）——恰恰相反，报纸是在"记录"（chronicling）危机，不是忽视。

❌ B 错误：failed to get state subsidies（未能获得国家补贴）——S05 提到 FTC 讨论"whether the state should subsidize them"，但那是讨论中，不是报纸已经失败。

❌ C 错误：were not charitable corporations（不是慈善公司）——S06 提到讨论"Should they become charitable corporations"，但这不是报纸困境的描述，而是解决方案的讨论。

27. Some newspapers refused delivery to distant suburbs probably because ______.
[A] they feared potential loss of readers [B] they were worried about increased costs [C] they wanted to concentrate on more profitable areas [D] they were not good at making delivery arrangements
参考答案：B

✅ 正确答案：B（they were worried about increased costs 担心成本增加）

定位：P3 · S14 — "Some papers even had the nerve to refuse delivery to distant suburbs" —— 结合上下文 S14 前文 "Readers are paying more for slimmer products"（读者多花钱买薄报）和 S13 "13,500 newsroom jobs have gone since 2007"（裁员省钱）。

推理：报纸在危机中大幅削减成本（裁员、缩版）。拒绝向远郊送报是明显的节省配送成本的手段——远郊配送成本高、订阅者少，不划算。B 选项 worried about increased costs 最合理。

❌ A 错误：feared potential loss of readers（担心失去读者）——拒绝送报恰恰是放弃远郊读者，不是怕失去他们。

❌ C 错误：concentrate on more profitable areas（聚焦更赚钱的地区）——虽然方向合理，但这是结果而非直接原因；核心动因是远郊配送成本高（costs）。

❌ D 错误：not good at delivery arrangements（不擅长配送安排）——与成本无关，文中未提配送能力问题。

28. By saying "Newspapers are becoming more balanced businesses" (Line 1, Para. 4), the author means that newspapers ______.
[A] are reducing their dependence on advertising [B] are becoming more profitable businesses [C] are relying more on readers' payments [D] are developing in a balanced way
参考答案：C

✅ 正确答案：C（are relying more on readers' payments 更依赖读者的付费）

定位：P4 · S16 — "Newspapers are becoming more balanced businesses, with a healthier mix of revenues from readers and advertisers"，结合 S14 "Readers are paying more for slimmer products" 和 S17 美国 87% vs 日本 35% 的数据对比。

推理："more balanced businesses" = 收入来源更平衡。过去美国报纸 87% 收入靠广告（过度依赖单一来源），现在读者付费（paying more）的比重上升，使收入结构更健康、更平衡。C 选项"更依赖读者付费"正是"更平衡"的核心含义。

❌ A 错误：reducing their dependence on advertising（减少对广告的依赖）——这是"更平衡"的结果之一，但表述太笼统。C 更具体地说明了新的收入来源（读者付费）。

❌ B 错误：becoming more profitable（变得更赚钱）——"balanced"强调结构健康，不是单纯盈利。

❌ D 错误：developing in a balanced way（以平衡的方式发展）——这是对"balanced"字面的同义重复，没有点出"收入来源变化"的实质。

29. Which of the following is TRUE according to the text?
[A] Newspapers are in trouble but not dying. [B] Newspaper advertising revenues are increasing. [C] The newspaper industry is losing more and more readers. [D] The newspaper industry is becoming more diversified.
参考答案：A

✅ 正确答案：A（Newspapers are in trouble but not dying 报纸处境艰难但没有消亡）

定位：全文主旨——P1 说危机逼近（in trouble），P2 说报纸 "not only survived but often returned to profit"（不但没死还盈利了）。

推理：文章的核心论点是"报纸没有死"。虽然经历了裁员、缩版等艰难措施（in trouble），但最终幸存并恢复盈利（not dying）。A 准确概括了全文主旨。

❌ B 错误：advertising revenues are increasing（广告收入在增加）——S17 说美国 87% 收入来自广告，但没有说广告收入在增加；反而强调报纸要减少对广告的依赖。

❌ C 错误：losing more and more readers（失去越来越多读者）——P1 提到读者流向互联网是背景，但全文重点是"幸存"，并未说读者持续流失。

❌ D 错误：becoming more diversified（变得更多元化）——文章说的是"更平衡"（balanced），不是"多元化"（diversified），两个概念不同。

30. The author's attitude toward the future of newspapers is ______.
[A] optimistic [B] pessimistic [C] neutral [D] uncertain
参考答案：A

✅ 正确答案：A（optimistic 乐观的）

定位：全文语气——P2 "have not only survived but often returned to profit"（不仅幸存还恢复盈利）、P4 "Newspapers are becoming more balanced businesses"（变得更平衡）、P5 "completeness is no longer a virtue"（说明报业在适应新环境）。

推理：虽然文中提到裁员等艰难措施（带着同情），但整体基调是积极的——报纸挺过了危机、恢复盈利、收入结构更健康。作者对报纸的未来持乐观态度。

❌ B 错误：pessimistic（悲观的）——与全文"幸存并恢复盈利"的主线相反。

❌ C 错误：neutral（中立的）——作者有明显的正面倾向，不是中立。

❌ D 错误：uncertain（不确定的）——作者态度明确（乐观），没有不确定性。
